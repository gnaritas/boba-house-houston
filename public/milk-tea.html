Milk Tea · Bubble Tea

Milk tea and bubble tea, shaken to order.

The heart of the Boba House counter — tea, shaken creamy, frosted with ice, finished with boba.

Milk tea at Boba House starts the same way every time: real tea pulled strong, shaken with milk and ice until the cup goes frosty. Classic, ube, matcha — the counter walks you through the build, sweetness your call.

Bubble tea is the house signature: the same shaken tea, tea-left or tea-heavy, with a spoonful of boba through the wide straw. Matcha lattes sit in the same family for the matcha regulars.

How people order it

The names off the Toast ordering page — Ube Creme and Pink Cloud — go over ice; Honey Lemon Tea is the iced side of the menu.

The Galleria-Uptown cup

Boba House shakes these cups at Suite 100 of The Conservatory Galleria, on West Alabama in the Galleria and Uptown stretch of Houston.

Milk tea questions

Before you order

Do everything come with tapioca pearls?

Bubble tea and milk tea are built with boba. Fruit tea and blended tea skip the pearls unless you ask.

Do you have matcha?

Yes — matcha tea and matcha lattes are on the counter.

Is the milk tea made to order?

Every cup is shaken fresh when you order it.
