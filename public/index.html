Opened with a lion dance

The shop that arrived on March 21, 2026

Boba House opened inside The Conservatory Galleria with a lion dance at 2 in the afternoon, and the Houston Press covered the whole first day — open from 11 in the morning until 9 at night, with a buy-one-get-one free milk tea for everyone who walked in.

The shop exists to be a cozy, welcoming place where every sip brings joy and satisfaction: a community of boba lovers, gathered one cup at a time.

Good questions

Asked most often

Do you cater events?

Yes — boba catering for parties, charity fundraisers and weddings. Call (832) 597-3205 or send a note from the catering page.

Are you open on Tuesdays?

No — the shop is closed on Tuesdays. It pours late on Friday and Saturday nights, until 11 pm.

Where exactly is Boba House?

5353 W Alabama St, Suite 100, inside The Conservatory Galleria food hall, Houston, TX 77056.
